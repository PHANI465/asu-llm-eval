ARIZONA STATE UNIVERSITY — CAMPUS AND ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

GENERAL INFO
- Founded: 1885
- Location: Greater Phoenix metropolitan area, Arizona
- Total enrollment: 140,000+ students
- Type: Large public research university
- Acceptance rate: ~89.9% (very accessible)
- Ranked #1 in innovation for multiple consecutive years

CAMPUSES
- Tempe Campus: Main campus, largest, most programs
- Downtown Phoenix Campus: Urban campus, business + public service focus
- Polytechnic Campus: Engineering, technology, aviation focus
- West Valley Campus (Glendale): Smaller, select programs
- Lake Havasu Campus: Smaller, select programs
- Los Angeles Campus: Select programs
- ASU Online: Majority online university as of Fall 2025
  No out-of-state tuition for online students

ACADEMIC PROGRAMS
- 350+ undergraduate majors
- 400+ undergraduate programs
- Masters and PhD programs across all major fields
