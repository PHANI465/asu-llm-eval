ARIZONA STATE UNIVERSITY — GRADUATE ADMISSIONS

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS
- Bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited US institution
  OR equivalent from an internationally recognized institution
- GPA: 3.00 on 4.00 scale in last 60 semester hours (typical expectation)
- Some programs may consider applicants below 3.0 GPA

ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS (vary by program)
- GRE or GMAT scores (program dependent — not universal)
- Letters of recommendation (typically 3)
- Statement of purpose / personal statement
- Resume or CV
- Writing samples or portfolio (program dependent)

NOTE: ASU does NOT accept GRE General Test taken at home

INTERNATIONAL GRADUATE APPLICANTS
- Same requirements as domestic + English proficiency proof
- Accepted tests: TOEFL, IELTS (check specific program for scores)
- All transcripts must be in original language + official English translation
- Photocopies and notarized transcripts NOT accepted

APPLICATION FEES
- US citizens / permanent residents / DACA / international online: $70
- International students on nonimmigrant visa (F-1, H1-B etc): $115

- Class registration begins: Late February 2026
- Classes begin: August 20, 2026
