ARIZONA STATE UNIVERSITY — SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL AID

INTERNATIONAL STUDENT SCHOLARSHIPS
- New American University (NAMU) Merit Scholarship:
  Auto-considered when you apply
  For international first-year and transfer F-1 students
  Awarded based on high academic achievement
  Can reduce costs by $15,000–$18,000/year for strong applicants

- College and Departmental Scholarships:
  Offered by individual colleges and departments
  Criteria and amounts vary
  Search at: asu.scholarshipuniverse.com

DOMESTIC STUDENT SCHOLARSHIPS
- Flinn Scholars Program:
  Full ride scholarship worth $135,000+
  Covers tuition, fees, housing, meals, study abroad
  For Arizona resident undergrads with academic excellence + leadership
  Highly competitive

- Barack Obama Scholars Program:
  Covers tuition, undergraduate college fees, student-initiated fees
  Plus a portion of housing, food, books, and supplies
  For eligible Arizona residents
  Up to 4 years

- Arizona Promise Scholarship:
  Helps eligible Arizona residents cover remaining tuition and fees
  After other aid is applied

- Western Undergraduate Exchange (WUE):
  For students from CA, NV, OR, WA, HI, AK, UT, NM, Guam and others
  Pay ~150% of in-state tuition instead of full non-resident rate
  Available at: Downtown Phoenix, Polytechnic, West Valley campuses
  IMPORTANT: WUE ends for new admits starting Fall 2026

- First-Generation Scholarships (College of Liberal Arts):
  Awards range from $750–$6,500/year
  For first-generation students in CLAS at Tempe campus
  Apply through ScholarshipUniverse

FEDERAL FINANCIAL AID
- International students do NOT qualify for federal/state aid
  (unless eligible noncitizen)
- Domestic students: Submit FAFSA by January 15 priority date
- ASU Federal School Code: 001081
- FAFSA used for grants, loans, Federal Work-Study, and scholarships

STUDENT EMPLOYMENT
- On-campus jobs available for all students
- International students can work on-campus
- View open positions at: students.asu.edu/employment/search

GENERAL NOTES
- 80% of ASU students receive some form of merit-based financial aid
- Average net price after grants: ~$16,400/year
- Use ASU Net Price Calculator for personalized estimate
