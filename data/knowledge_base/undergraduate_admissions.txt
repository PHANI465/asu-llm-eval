ARIZONA STATE UNIVERSITY — UNDERGRADUATE ADMISSIONS

GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
- 4 years Math
- 4 years English (non-ESL/ELL)
- 3 years Lab Sciences (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Earth Science)
- 2 years Social Sciences (including 1 year American History)
- 2 years same Second Language
- 1 year Fine Arts OR Career & Technical Education

APTITUDE REQUIREMENTS
Students must meet ONE of the following academic criteria:
- Top 25% in high school graduating class
- 3.00+ unweighted GPA in competency courses (4.0 scale)
- Qualifying SAT score (see SAT AND ACT SCORES section below)
- Qualifying ACT score (see SAT AND ACT SCORES section below)

SAT AND ACT SCORES
- SAT and ACT scores are NOT required for admission to ASU
- They may be submitted optionally for:
  * Course placement
  * Satisfying aptitude requirements as an alternative to GPA or class rank
- If submitting SAT scores, the minimum threshold for Arizona residents is 1120
- If submitting SAT scores, the minimum threshold for non-residents is 1180
- If submitting ACT scores, the minimum threshold for Arizona residents is 22
- If submitting ACT scores, the minimum threshold for non-residents is 24

NOTE: Some programs (Engineering, Business, Barrett Honors) have higher requirements

APPLICATION FEES
- Arizona residents: $50
- Domestic non-residents: $80
- ASU Online: $70
- International students: $85

IMPORTANT DEADLINES (Fall 2026)
- Application opens: July 1 each year
- Priority admission date: November 1
- Regular admission date: January 15
- FAFSA priority date: January 15

HOW TO APPLY
- Apply via ASU Application for Admission OR Common Application
- No essay or personal statement required for first-year students
- Self-report high school grades for faster decision
- ACT/SAT scores optional but can be submitted for course placement

SPECIAL PATHWAYS
- Earned Admission: Complete 24 semester credits with 2.75 GPA
- Transfer with 24+ college credits and 2.50 cumulative GPA
- GED accepted: score 170+ (post-2013 tests)
